# Suggestions

Ideas noticed during work, not yet asked for. Strike or promote as you see fit.

- **The nice!nano's board edge clears the tray ledge by only 0.03 mm.** With the nano under the board (`U1`, flipped), its PCB reaches to 0.63 mm inside the board's north edge, while the 1.0 mm ledge minus the 0.4 mm gap reaches 0.6 mm under the board edge (`scripts/export_tray.py`, `DEFAULT_LEDGE_WIDTH_MM`, `DEFAULT_GAP_MM`). The USB window removes the ledge over its 14 mm width, but the nano is 17.8 mm wide, so 2 mm at each end still face the ledge at 3.85 to 5.25 mm above the floor. Options: a narrower ledge (`ledge_width=0.9` or less), no ledge along that stretch, or the nano 0.3 mm further south on the board. Measured on LibrePCB's populated STEP export on 2026-09-27; not changed because it is a design choice between the ledge and the nano's position.
- **The west column's hotswap socket tabs end 0.17 mm from the tray wall.** Each PG1350 socket tab reaches 0.23 mm past the board outline (LibrePCB's placement of the package model, measured on the populated STEP export, 2026-10-08) and the wall sits `gap` = 0.4 mm outside the outline (`scripts/export_tray.py`, `DEFAULT_GAP_MM`). The ledge notches clear the tabs, but a print that comes out a little tight will rub there first. `gap=0.5` on the command line widens the whole cavity by 0.1 mm; the alternative is a shallow recess in the wall at the four notches. Left alone because the printed tray has not been tried yet.
- **The plate STL is not a closed mesh.** `scripts/export_plate.py` meshes the plate as a compound of touching cell solids (plus the tab and now the corner slab), so the STL carries coincident internal walls at every cell boundary: FreeCAD reports it non-manifold with hundreds of self-intersections, for the July 2026 export and the current one alike (the tray and PCB replica STLs are clean). Slicers have evidently coped so far. Fusing the solids once (`multiFuse` plus `removeSplitter`, which `drill_holes` already does for its connectivity check) before `export_stl` would give a single closed shell. Noticed 2026-10-08 while verifying the corner extension; left alone because it changes the shipped mesh of a part that already prints.
- **The printed plate's holes drift about 1 mm toward the nice!nano side over the plate's width; the cause is not found.** Checked 2026-10-09: `boards/board_1.1/board.lp` has every switch pitch at exactly 19.05 mm, the committed Excellon files put the 3.4 mm switch drills and the 2.4 mm mounting drills at the same positions, the kbdkid3 plate model's cutouts match the switch grid to 0.0005 mm, the exported plate STL has every cutout, screw hole and the reset hole at its design x within 0.08 mm, and the tray STL's standoff bores sit at the mounting drills to 0.001 mm. Both parts were printed unscaled. A 1 mm drift at 125 mm is 0.8 %, the size of a print scale or shrinkage error, but a plate shrunk that much should sit loose in the tray, which it does not. `pattern_shift=0.5` in `scripts/export_plate.py` now splits the error empirically. To find the cause, measure: the printed plate's width (design 135.05 mm) and the span from the left edge of the bottom-left cutout to the right edge of the thumb cutout (design 128.4 mm), the PCB's two bottom mounting holes centre to centre (design 95.57 mm), and the printed tray's outer width (design 137.02 mm).
