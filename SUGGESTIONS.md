# Suggestions

Ideas noticed during work, not yet asked for. Strike or promote as you see fit.

- **The populated board binds on the tray's ledge; the nice!nano is the tightest spot.** Measured 2026-10-11 with FreeCAD on LibrePCB's populated STEP export against the trays `scripts/export_tray.py` builds, board resting on the standoffs (probe in `tmp/check_fit.py`, not committed): no part cuts into the nominal tray, but the clearances are smaller than FDM swelling. Left half: the nano's north edge is 0.03 mm from the ledge at both ends of the USB window (the window clears 14 mm of a 17.8 mm wide nano), with the nano's PCB 2.75 to 4.15 mm below the board, so a nano that touches the ledge holds the board 2.75 mm above its seat; the nano's east edge is about 0.27 mm from the east ledge over its whole length; the west socket tabs (S100 to S103) end 0.17 mm from the wall inside their notches; the bottom-row sockets (S113, S123, S133, S143, S153, S163) are 0.38 mm from the ledge along the stepped south edge; socket S150 is 0.50 mm from the standoff at (94.77, 60.40), S141 0.67 mm from the one at (76.04, 43.26), J1 0.76 mm from the one at (114.14, 19.77); the power switch S1 has its 0.5 mm window margin. Right half: the nano (U2) numbers are the same, and socket S253 comes 0.15 mm from the ledge's rounded concave corner at the south-edge step near x 37.5 (the well's concave corners get corner radius plus ledge width, 2.13 mm, so the ledge bulges inward there). Candidate fixes, by expected effect: cut the ledge away along the north edge over the nano's full width (the USB window's ledge cut from 14 to about 20 mm, or no ledge between the switch window and the east corner) and relieve it along the east edge beside the nano, or move the nano 0.3 mm south on the board; `gap=0.5` or `ledge_width=0.9` on the command line for 0.1 mm more everywhere; a shallow recess into the wall at the four west notches. Measuring the printed cavity between the ledge faces (design 133.42 mm at the board's full width) would size the swelling to design against.
- **The plate STL is not a closed mesh.** `scripts/export_plate.py` meshes the plate as a compound of touching cell solids (plus the tab and now the corner slab), so the STL carries coincident internal walls at every cell boundary: FreeCAD reports it non-manifold with hundreds of self-intersections, for the July 2026 export and the current one alike (the tray and PCB replica STLs are clean). Slicers have evidently coped so far. Fusing the solids once (`multiFuse` plus `removeSplitter`, which `drill_holes` already does for its connectivity check) before `export_stl` would give a single closed shell. Noticed 2026-10-08 while verifying the corner extension; left alone because it changes the shipped mesh of a part that already prints.
- **The printed plate's holes drift about 1 mm toward the nice!nano side over the plate's width; the cause is not found.** Checked 2026-10-09: `boards/board_1.1/board.lp` has every switch pitch at exactly 19.05 mm, the committed Excellon files put the 3.4 mm switch drills and the 2.4 mm mounting drills at the same positions, the kbdkid3 plate model's cutouts match the switch grid to 0.0005 mm, the exported plate STL has every cutout, screw hole and the reset hole at its design x within 0.08 mm, and the tray STL's standoff bores sit at the mounting drills to 0.001 mm. Both parts were printed unscaled. A 1 mm drift at 125 mm is 0.8 %, the size of a print scale or shrinkage error, but a plate shrunk that much should sit loose in the tray, which it does not. `pattern_shift` in `scripts/export_plate.py` corrects it empirically: a 0.5 mm shift came out offset about as far the other way (printed 2026-10-10; a linear 1 mm drift would have left both ends 0.5 mm off in opposite directions instead), so the default is 0.25 mm now. To find the cause, measure: the printed plate's width (design 135.05 mm for the plates printed so far, 134.85 mm since the side shave of 2026-10-10) and the span from the left edge of the bottom-left cutout to the right edge of the thumb cutout (design 128.4 mm), the PCB's two bottom mounting holes centre to centre (design 95.57 mm), and the printed tray's outer width (design 137.02 mm).
